What watering frequency should I use?

Most houseplants: 5–14 days. Cacti and succulents: 14–28 days. Tropicals (monstera, peace lily): 5–7 days. Adjust based on light, pot size, season, and your home's humidity. The hint in the app gives a starting point — soil-finger-test will give the actual answer.
